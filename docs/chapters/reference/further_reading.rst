===============
Further reading
===============

This page collects broader references and practical resources related to
imaging transcriptomics beyond the toolbox itself.

Foundational and practical papers
---------------------------------

- Arnatkeviciute, A., Fulcher, B. D. & Fornito, A. A practical guide to linking
  brain-wide gene expression and neuroimaging data. *NeuroImage* **189**,
  353-367 (2019). `https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.01.011
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.01.011>`_

- Markello, R. D. *et al.* Standardizing workflows in imaging transcriptomics
  with the abagen toolbox. *eLife* **10**, e72129 (2021).
  `https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.72129
  <https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.72129>`_

Spatial nulls and enrichment
----------------------------

- Markello, R. D. & Misic, B. Comparing spatial null models for brain maps.
  *NeuroImage* **236**, 118052 (2021).
  `https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2021.118052
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2021.118052>`_

- Fulcher, B. D., Arnatkeviciute, A. & Fornito, A. Overcoming false-positive
  gene-category enrichment in the human brain. *Nature Communications* **12**,
  2669 (2021). `https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-22862-1
  <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-22862-1>`_

Reviews and perspective
